Everything You Need to Know

Helpful information to prepare for your visit and stay at Jackson Hospital.

Visiting Hours

  • General Wards: 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM
  • ICU: 11:00 AM – 1:00 PM & 4:00 PM – 6:00 PM
  • Maternity: Flexible with staff approval
  • Children under 12: Accompanied by adult

Before Your Visit

  • Bring valid ID and insurance card
  • List of current medications
  • Previous medical records if available
  • Arrive 15–20 minutes early
  • Follow any fasting instructions

Insurance & Billing

  • We accept most major insurance plans
  • Self-pay options available
  • Transparent pricing estimates
  • Financial counseling available
  • Contact billing for questions

Patient Rights

  • Right to respectful, quality care
  • Right to privacy and confidentiality
  • Right to informed consent
  • Right to access medical records
  • Right to voice concerns

What to Bring

  • Comfortable clothing & toiletries
  • Phone charger and personal items
  • List of emergency contacts
  • Advance directives if applicable
  • Reading materials or entertainment

Important Contacts

  • Billing: Ext. 2200

Patient Safety is Our Priority

Jackson Hospital follows strict infection control protocols and patient safety guidelines. Our staff is trained in the latest safety practices to protect you and your loved ones during every visit.

  • Hand hygiene stations throughout the facility
  • Regular facility sanitization
  • Clear patient identification protocols
  • Medication safety checks
