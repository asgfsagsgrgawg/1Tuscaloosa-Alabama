You can book an appointment online through our Appointment page, call us at +1 325 430 7617, or visit the hospital reception. Online booking is available 24/7 and our team will confirm your slot shortly after submission.

General ward visiting hours are from 10:00 AM to 8:00 PM. ICU has limited hours (11:00 AM–1:00 PM and 4:00 PM–6:00 PM). Maternity and special units may have flexible arrangements. Please check with the ward staff for specific guidelines.

Yes, Jackson Hospital accepts most major insurance plans. We recommend contacting your insurance provider and our billing department to verify coverage before your visit. Self-pay and payment plan options are also available.

Please bring a valid photo ID, your insurance card, a list of current medications, any relevant previous medical records or test results, and your referral letter if applicable. Arrive 15–20 minutes early to complete any necessary paperwork.

Yes. Our Emergency Department operates 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including holidays. For life-threatening emergencies, call emergency services immediately or come directly to our ED. You can also reach us at +1 325 430 7617.

You may request copies of your medical records through the Medical Records Department. A formal request form and valid identification are required. Processing typically takes 5–7 business days. Contact us for assistance or more details.
